📉 Task 2:
 Unemployment Analysis in India

📌 Project Overview:
 Explores unemployment across Indian states and shows how COVID-19 affected it.

🛠️ Tech Stack and Libraries: 
 Python, Jupyter Notebook, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn

🔄 Workflow and Implementation:
 Load and clean data → Region-wise and month-wise EDA → Time-series chart → Top 10 states → Correlation heatmap → Pre-COVID vs post-COVID comparison

📊 Key Results:
 Unemployment rose sharply after the March 2020 lockdown and peaked in April and May 2020. Some states were hit much harder than others.
