🚗 Task 3: 
Car Price Prediction

📌 Project Overview:
 Predicts the selling price of a used car from its brand, age, kilometres driven, fuel type and transmission.

🛠️ Tech Stack and Libraries: 
 Python, Jupyter Notebook, pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn, scikit-learn

🔄 Workflow and Implementation: 
Data cleaning → Feature engineering (car age and brand) → EDA → Encoding → Correlation heatmap → 80/20 split → Train 3 regression models → Evaluate with MAE, RMSE and R² → Feature importance

📊 Model Performance: 
Random Forest and Gradient Boosting performed better than Linear Regression, with higher R² and lower error. Car age and kilometres driven were the key features.
